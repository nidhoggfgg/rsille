# rsille 3.4.2

Eliminates Kitty graphics flicker when floating overlays over alternate-screen
apps such as Vim and Neovim.

- `rsille-render 0.1.3`: `KittyImage` double-buffers image uploads so the new
  image is transmitted and placed before deleting the previous one, and adds
  `KittyImage::place_sized` for lightweight cell-scaled re-placement.
- `rsille-overlay 0.1.2`: defers child synchronized-update resets (`?2026l`)
  until after overlay output in both single- and multi-batch frames, buffers
  session writes, adds `Overlay::invalidate_placements`, and refreshes
  alternate-screen placements immediately on scroll or clear sequences without
  re-uploading pixels on ordinary cursor/text updates.
